## Project Overview

This project was conducted in collaboration with Woodward Inc. as part of an industrial Research & Development (R&D) initiative focused on the development and optimization of a Free-Piston Linear Generator (FPLG).

My work focused on prototype troubleshooting, mechanical redesign, structural analysis, and design optimization to improve system functionality, manufacturability, and assembly efficiency.

## My Contributions

### Prototype Development & Troubleshooting

- Restored a non-functional compressed-air prototype to stable operation at **100 cycles/min** through root-cause analysis and mechanical refinement.
- Identified and resolved alignment, sealing, and timing-related issues to improve prototype functionality.

### Mechanical Design & Structural Analysis

- Developed a streamlined mechanical architecture through component redesign and assembly optimization in SolidWorks.
- Performed static structural analysis using ANSYS to evaluate component stresses and support design decisions.
- Applied precision engineering and tolerancing principles to improve component alignment and integration.

### Design for Manufacturing & Assembly

- Achieved a **24% estimated reduction in assembly time**, equivalent to **26 minutes per assembly**, through DFM/DFA principles.
- Simplified component architecture, standardized fastening hardware, and incorporated symmetric design features to improve assembly efficiency.
